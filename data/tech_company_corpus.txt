OpenAI was founded in 2015 by Sam Altman, Elon Musk, Greg Brockman, Ilya Sutskever, John Schulman, and Wojciech Zaremba. OpenAI develops large language models such as GPT-3, GPT-4, and GPT-4o.

Microsoft invested in OpenAI and integrated OpenAI models into Azure AI, Microsoft Copilot, GitHub Copilot, and Bing Chat.

Google was founded by Larry Page and Sergey Brin in 1998. Google developed products such as Google Search, Gmail, Android, YouTube, and Google Cloud.

DeepMind was founded by Demis Hassabis, Shane Legg, and Mustafa Suleyman. Google acquired DeepMind in 2014. DeepMind developed AlphaGo and AlphaFold.

Meta was founded by Mark Zuckerberg. Meta develops Llama, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, and Reality Labs.

Anthropic was founded by Dario Amodei and Daniela Amodei. Anthropic develops Claude and focuses on AI safety.

Amazon was founded by Jeff Bezos. Amazon operates AWS, which provides cloud computing services and AI infrastructure.

NVIDIA was founded by Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky, and Curtis Priem. NVIDIA develops GPUs used for AI training and inference.

Tesla was founded by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning. Elon Musk later became CEO of Tesla. Tesla develops electric vehicles, Autopilot, and robotics systems.

Apple was founded by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne. Apple develops iPhone, macOS, iOS, and Apple Intelligence.

GitHub was acquired by Microsoft in 2018. GitHub provides code hosting and developer collaboration tools. GitHub Copilot is powered by OpenAI models.

YouTube was acquired by Google in 2006. YouTube is a video-sharing platform and is integrated with Google advertising services.

Android was acquired by Google in 2005. Android became a major mobile operating system used by many smartphone manufacturers.

LinkedIn was acquired by Microsoft in 2016. LinkedIn is a professional social network and uses AI for job recommendations.

Hugging Face was founded by Clément Delangue, Julien Chaumond, and Thomas Wolf. Hugging Face provides open-source machine learning models and datasets.

Mistral AI was founded in France by Arthur Mensch, Guillaume Lample, and Timothée Lacroix. Mistral AI develops open-weight large language models.

Perplexity AI develops an AI-powered answer engine that combines search with large language models.

Databricks was founded by the creators of Apache Spark. Databricks provides a lakehouse platform for data engineering, analytics, and machine learning.

Snowflake provides a cloud data platform used for data warehousing, analytics, and AI workloads.

Oracle develops database systems and cloud infrastructure. Oracle Cloud supports enterprise applications and AI workloads.
